FAQ

Common questions answered

How long does company incorporation take? +

With complete documentation, SSM online incorporation takes 1–3 working days. Timeline depends on entity type and completeness of documents — we keep you updated at every step.

Do I need to be in Malaysia to register a company? +

No. The entire process can be completed remotely. We handle document submission, SSM correspondence, and provide a digital copy of your incorporation certificate.

Why do I need a company secretary? +

The Companies Act 2016 requires every Malaysian company to have a qualified company secretary. BIJAK I provides licensed company secretaries for all our clients as part of our ongoing service.
