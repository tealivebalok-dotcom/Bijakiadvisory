## Key Strategic Findings

### 1. THE BLANK SPACE
**No major company secretarial firm is dominating Facebook with direct-to-SME content.**

The landscape has:
- Government pages (SSM, MIDA, SME Corp) — announcements only, no interaction
- Big 4 (KPMG, Baker Tilly) — corporate content, too expensive for SMEs
- Professional bodies (MIA, ACCA) — member-focused, not SME-owner focused
- Chambers (FMM, MICCI) — industry advocacy, not operational help

**BIJAK can own the space between "government announcement" and "Big 4 corporate" — the hungry SME owner who needs clear, honest, affordable answers.**

### 2. CONTENT GAP ANALYSIS

| Content Type | SSM | Big 4 | Prof Bodies | **BIJAK Opportunity** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "How to register" guides | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | **✅ Sharp, step-by-step** |
| Hidden cost warnings | ❌ | Partial | ❌ | **✅ Name and shame the tricks** |
| Foreign ownership explained simply | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | **✅ Direct, sector-specific** |
| Myth-busting (compliance) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | **✅ Weekly myth buster** |
| Same-day registration reality | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | **✅ "What must be ready" checklist** |
| Annual compliance calendar | ❌ | Partial | ❌ | **✅ Annual deadline reminders** |
| Transparent pricing | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | **✅ Fixed-fee positioning** |
| Direct Q&A / engagement | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | **✅ Respond within 1 hour** |

### 3. WHAT WORKS ON THESE PAGES (from observation)

**What gets engagement:**
- Breaking news about regulatory changes (SSM new rules, tax updates)
- Deadline reminders (annual return deadlines, tax filing deadlines)
- Myth-busting content (contrarian, "you're doing it wrong")
- Pain-point content ("X hidden cost that will surprise you")
- "What you need to know before [action]" posts

**What gets NO engagement:**
- Generic "We are pleased to announce..." posts
- Pure promotional content ("Our services include...")
- Long-form technical content (reserved for websites/LinkedIn)
- Stock photo posts with no hook

### 4. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR BIJAK PAGE

**Do:**
- ✅ Sharp pain-first hooks in every post
- ✅ Comment-gating ("Comment X for Y checklist") to boost algorithm
- ✅ Fast response to all comments/DMs (1 hour target)
- ✅ Use Malay language hooks occasionally (mix EN/MY)
- ✅ Behind-the-scenes of real company registrations (with client permission)
- ✅ Share real client wins (testimonials with permission)
- ✅ Post on consistent schedule (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat)

**Don't:**
- ❌ Don't post SSM announcements as your own (SSM does this better)
- ❌ Don't use generic stock photos — use real team photos
- ❌ Don't write long paragraphs — 2-3 sentences max
- ❌ Don't sound like a government page or a Big 4 firm
- ❌ Don't be vague — namespecific costs, timelines, penalties

**Page Aesthetic:**
- Navy (#1B2B4B) as primary color — professional, trustworthy
- Gold (#C9A84C) for highlights and callouts
- Clean white background
- Professional headshot photos for team
- Consistent template for all posts (use Canva)
