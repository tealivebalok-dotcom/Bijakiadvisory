Can a foreigner fully own a company in Malaysia? +

Yes. In most industries, Malaysia allows 100% foreign ownership for Sdn Bhd companies — no local partner required. Some sectors (e.g., banking, defence, legal) have restrictions. Our team will advise on your specific industry within the first free consultation.

How long does it take to incorporate a company in Malaysia? +

With complete documentation, SSM online incorporation is handled efficiently. Timeline depends on entity type and completeness of documents — we keep you updated at every milestone and work toward the fastest possible registration.

Do I need to travel to Malaysia to set up my company? +

No. The entire process can be completed remotely. We handle document submission, SSM correspondence, and can provide a digital copy of your incorporation certificate. For documents requiring notarisation in your home country, we guide you on the steps — usually a simple apostille or notarisation at your local Malaysian embassy.

What documents do I need to incorporate a company? +

The basic requirements include: passport/IC copies of directors/shareholders, proof of residential address, proposed company name, and share capital details. For foreign nationals, additional documents may be needed depending on your country of origin. We provide a tailored checklist during our first consultation.

Why do I need a company secretary in Malaysia? +

The Companies Act 2016 requires every Malaysian company to have at least one qualified company secretary who is a Malaysian citizen or permanent resident. The company secretary ensures your company stays compliant with SSM, files annual returns on time, maintains proper statutory records, and advises the board on governance matters. BIJAK I provides licensed company secretaries for all our clients as part of our ongoing service.

What is the difference between Sdn Bhd and LLP? +

An Sdn Bhd (Sendirian Berhad) is a private limited company with shareholders whose liability is limited to their share capital. An LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) is a partnership structure where partners have limited liability but with more flexibility in management. LLPs are popular for professional services firms. Sdn Bhd is generally preferred for trading, investment, and operational businesses.

Annual maintenance costs (company secretary, accounting, tax filing) vary based on transaction volume and business complexity. We share full pricing upfront before any engagement begins.
